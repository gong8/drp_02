Collated feedback from each of your markers

Prioritise Fixing

Things that need urgent attention before next week.

Try to set your problem via a user-story for a realistic person, not just "some young people". Your opportunity statement still needs work - its a bit too wordy (the "small tweaks" part is probably no needed).

"Young adults" remains too broad a definition. People in this group have vastly different experiences and motivations. Define your target audience more precisely. Your HMW also needs to be developed and sharpened every week. The value your service delivers needs to be stated more clearly within it. Step back from individual features and refocus on how users actually interact with the service as a whole and what they get from it.

To Improve

Things you should improve moving forward (likely for next week, but may be something more specific), or something you should consider looking into.

You are working on perhaps too many interfaces at once in your mock-ups. Try to focus more on user needs and less on features. You also need to get out to a wider range of test users (get away from DoC students).

Bring your persona back into the presentation to ground the problem in a human story from the start. Your user testing is currently too feature-focused. Try shifting the focus towards understanding user needs and the value the service delivers. Also think about the onboarding experience for new users, as this is often where services lose people early.

Keep Doing

Things you did well in this milestone: keep it going!

Good to see detailed refinement of your core interaction via the mock-ups. Nice walking skeleton that get the key point of your project across.

The thoroughness of your initial ideation was clear. It is also evident that you have been thinking about how to deliver core value through your service rather than just building features.
