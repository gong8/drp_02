# #78 — paper mockups

`source: edstem thread #78`

**Question:** A student asked whether physical paper mockups could be passed directly to assessors during the assessment, or whether they had to be scanned and presented digitally.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Either format (physical paper or scanned/digital) is fine. However, materials should be well organised so that no time is wasted shuffling through paper or slides — each group only has 20 minutes, and the goal is to maximise time spent discussing work and receiving feedback. If presenting multiple iterations, cherry-pick the 2-3 most impactful changes rather than walking through all 4-5, as that would take too long. Assessment is based on the quality of work and interviews/feedback, not the quantity.
