# #17 — Days of meetings

`source: edstem thread #17`

**Question:** A student asks whether the day of the weekly meetings is fixed or whether there is flexibility to suggest a different day if needed.

**Answer (Jamie Willis):** Meetings will always be on Fridays.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The day of the weekly milestone review sessions is non-negotiable, and the time slots within Fridays are also highly constrained (details to be explained the following week). Mondays will have weekly lectures and clinic hours, but all weekly milestone assessments take place on Fridays. The final presentations are scheduled for Tuesday or Wednesday of week 8 of the term (16th or 17th June).
