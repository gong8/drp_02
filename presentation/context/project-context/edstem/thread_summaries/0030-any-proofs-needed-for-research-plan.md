# #30 — Any proofs needed for research plan?

`source: edstem thread #30`

**Question:** A student asked whether research conducted for the research plan (e.g. interviews) needs to be supported by evidence such as video or audio recordings, or whether a written summary in their own words is sufficient.

**Answer (Zaki Amin):** Evidence should always be sought, ideally in video or audio clips, as summaries alone come across as hearsay. It is fine — and encouraged — to curate and paraphrase what interviewees say to extract only the relevant parts, but teams should seek permission from interviewees to obtain real testimony. Evidence does not need to be included wholesale in the milestone submission; rather, teams should be ready to present the most important insights and supporting evidence (e.g. in a slideshow or Notion page) in whichever format they choose.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Evidence must be presentable during each week's assessment deliverable — the Elevator Pitch in the current week, and Milestone Review sessions in subsequent weeks. Teams are not expected to present all evidence, but something must be shown to back up their analysis and decision-making, ideally curated to the most impactful snippets of conversation or feedback.
