# #20 — DRP Project Preparations

`source: edstem thread #20`

**Question:** Staff announcement addressing how students should prepare for the upcoming DRP project week and what to expect in the opening days.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The DRP project kicks off on Monday 18th May with a Welcome Session at 10:00 in lecture theatre 308, followed by a Q&A on assessment criteria until 12:00, then an RCA-led lecture on Storytelling. Key details:

- **DRP Clinic Hours** (individual group support) run 14:00–16:00 in the Level 2 labs on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday; coordinated via the COMP50010 Teams channel.
- Additional Human-Centred Design Lectures are scheduled on Tuesday 19th May and Wednesday 20th May mornings.
- By end of week, groups must have chosen a **problem** (not a solution) and pitch it on **Friday 22nd May** (further details to follow early next week).
- To prepare now: review the DRP Assessment Template Document (final version due by start of next week); brainstorm problems (not products/apps) drawing on UN Sustainability Goals or past project examples; experiment with web/app development frameworks discussed in thread #18.
