# #112 — Introduction Pitch vs Milestone 1 video

`source: edstem thread #112`

**Question:** A student asked how the opening introduction pitch of the final presentation differs from the Milestone 1 elevator pitch video (beyond the mini-demo), and whether footage from the Milestone 1 video could be reused.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** You are allowed to reuse parts of your Milestone 1 Elevator Pitch in the Final Presentation opening, but most groups will likely have a much deeper understanding of their problem context than they did three weeks ago, so significant reworking of any pre-recorded content is probably warranted. Importantly, there is no requirement to include any pre-recorded content at all.
