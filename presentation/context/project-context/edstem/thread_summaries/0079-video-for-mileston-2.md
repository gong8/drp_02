# #79 — Video for Mileston 2

`source: edstem thread #79`

**Question:** A student asked whether submitting a video (as done in Milestone 1) would still be acceptable for Milestone 2, noting they understood a conversational approach was preferred.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** No, videos are not accepted for Milestone 2. The milestone requires a live conversation/discussion so that staff can ask questions and redirect the discussion as needed. A video could go off on a tangent and waste students' time, whereas a conversation keeps things on-topic. Staff also noted this approach saves students the effort of producing a video every week.
