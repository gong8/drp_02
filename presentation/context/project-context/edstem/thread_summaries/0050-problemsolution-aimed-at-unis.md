# #50 — Problem/Solution Aimed at Unis

`source: edstem thread #50`

**Question:** A student asks whether it is acceptable to pivot their project's stakeholders to university students and support staff (keeping the same underlying problem) after receiving feedback suggesting this change — and whether this would fall foul of the warning against solving problems faced by DoC students.

**Answer (Jamie Willis):** Yes, this is allowed. The reason DoC students are discouraged as stakeholders is that they are too close to the problem and their technical background biases their feedback, undermining the quality of iteration DRP requires — not because university students in general are off-limits.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Agreed; targeting non-DoC students specifically helps avoid the bias issue.
