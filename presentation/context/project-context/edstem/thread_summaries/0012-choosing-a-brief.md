# #12 — Choosing a brief

`source: edstem thread #12`

**Question:** A student asked whether groups are required to choose one of the three provided briefs for their DRP project, noting their group had developed strong ideas outside those briefs.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** For the workshop on the day of the post, groups were expected to choose one of the three briefs from the preparation slides. However, for the project proper, there is complete freedom of choice over the problem context. He also reminded students that the initial focus should be on identifying a specific real-world problem and understanding user stories and needs before moving to solutions.
