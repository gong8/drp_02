# #72 — Feedback/validation of concept without mock-ups

`source: edstem thread #72`

**Question:** A student asked whether verbal stakeholder feedback (gathered before any mock-ups existed) counts as feedback/validation of concept for Milestone 2, and whether it should be included in the presentation given it may not be formally assessed.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The verbal feedback would not count toward assessment of mock-ups or how they were used, but the criteria for quality of feedback is independent of whether mock-ups were involved. If the feedback was significant and impactful on the project direction, it should be included. However, make sure the presentation also includes material/evidence addressing the other assessment criteria.
