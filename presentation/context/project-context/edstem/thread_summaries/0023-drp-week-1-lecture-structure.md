# #23 — DRP - Week 1 Lecture Structure

`source: edstem thread #23`

**Question:** Staff announcement outlining the full teaching schedule for DRP Week 1 (18–21 May), so students know what sessions to expect and when.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The Week 1 schedule is as follows:

- **Monday 18 May**
  - 10:00–12:00 (HXLY 308): Welcome Talk — full DRP structure, requirements, expectations, Milestone 0 advice, and Milestone 1 (Friday 22 May) assessment criteria; open Q&A from ~11:00.
  - 12:00–13:00 (HXLY 308): HCD Lecture — Storytelling (communicating innovative ideas through narrative).
  - 14:00–16:00 (Level 2 labs / Teams COMP50010): Clinic Hours — discuss project ideas with DRP Tutor Team (DoC, RCA staff, RCA Service Design students). Teams must have completed a Research Plan from the DRP Week 1 Playbook before seeking help.
- **Tuesday 19 May**
  - 11:00–12:30 (HXLY 308): HCD Lecture — Testing Ideas and Digital Prototyping (overview of prototype types for different design stages).
  - 14:00–16:00 (Level 2 labs / Teams COMP50010): Clinic Hours.
- **Wednesday 20 May**
  - 10:00–11:30 (HXLY 308): HCD Lecture — User Experience (how users interact with and experience a product).
  - 14:00–16:00 (Level 2 labs / Teams COMP50010): Clinic Hours.
- **Thursday 21 May**
  - 14:00–16:00 (Level 2 labs / Teams COMP50010): Clinic Hours.
