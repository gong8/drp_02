# #102 — How to select what evidence to showcase

`source: edstem thread #102`

**Question:** With multiple interviews and feedback items collected across iterations, is it acceptable to omit some feedback from the presentation and simply tell tutors verbally what was found, rather than showcasing everything?

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Being selective is expected and necessary — there will not be enough time to show all feedback or even all changes made. Focus on the most impactful or insightful feedback, since quality matters more than volume. Non-impactful or "fairly obvious" changes/feedback should be left out unless spare time allows for them (as also discussed in the Milestone 3 advice from thread #63).
