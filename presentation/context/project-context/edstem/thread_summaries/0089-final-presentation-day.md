# #89 — Final Presentation Day

`source: edstem thread #89`

**Question:** A student asked whether the final presentation occurs on both Monday and Tuesday, or just one of those days, and when groups would find out which day their presentation is scheduled.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Each group has only one final presentation, and groups will have some choice over which day/time slot they take. Key details:
- A booking spreadsheet tab will be opened once assessment stream allocations from the Milestone 2-4 tab have been copied over.
- Groups must stay within the assessment stream determined by their allocated DRP Tutor Pair, but all streams span both days.
- An EdStem announcement with a direct link to the correct spreadsheet tab and a clear booking open date/time will be posted to give all groups a chance to view the schedule before bookings go live.
- The announcement was expected later that week (likely the next day), with bookings anticipated to open sometime Wednesday afternoon.
