# #4 — Introduction to Human-Centred Design - Interactive Lectures

`source: edstem thread #4`

**Question:** This is a staff announcement about two upcoming interactive lectures on Human-Centred Design (HCD) and the supporting materials available.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Two live, interactive HCD lectures will be held in HXLY 308, taught by Nick de Leon (Executive Education and Knowledge Exchange Lead, Royal College of Art School of Design):

- **Wednesday 18th March, 11:00–13:00**
- **Thursday 19th March, 11:00–13:00**

Topics covered include: introduction to design and creative methods; the HCD approach and its relevance to ICT/HCI; key HCD principles with case studies; design methodologies; HCD research methods (rapid ethnography, personas, design interventions); and developing insights and opportunity statements/design briefs for digital touchpoints.

A further on-site **workshop (all day Friday)** will also follow — details to be posted separately.

Slides will be uploaded to the [COMP50010 Scientia materials page](https://scientia.doc.ic.ac.uk/2526/modules/50010/materials) once received; a [3-minute RCA video overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5w6kTK_BgM) is available in the meantime. Attendance is strongly encouraged. The Wednesday HCD slides were subsequently confirmed as uploaded to Scientia.
