# #47 — How does the coming week work?

`source: edstem thread #47`

**Question:** A student asked whether Milestone 2 is feasible given that the project overview lecture isn't until Tuesday, leaving only roughly 2.5 days, and requested that the overview slides (or last year's lecture) be shared early.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):**
- The May Bank Holiday compresses the week for everyone equally, but the milestone is achievable in the time given — not a lot of code needs to be written.
- The overview slides are not shareable ahead of Tuesday because adjustments are still being made, and the slides are not meaningful without the accompanying presentation.
- The week's focus is on **concept development**, though most groups will also need to revisit background research and their problem/opportunity statement (these should evolve continuously).
- Last week's lectures on "prototyping a digital service" are especially relevant this week.
- Groups are encouraged to meet with stakeholders/users regularly, ideally a mixture of new and previous users.
- The Milestone 2 Assessment Template centres on getting user feedback on solution prototypes — groups are allowed to start thinking about solutions, provided they have a solid understanding of their core problem.
- Full details will be covered in Tuesday morning's overview session.
- The slides for the weekly DRP Overview lecture were posted to Scientia on 26 May.
