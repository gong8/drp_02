# #100 — Where are the examples of HCD data gathering methods in the playbook?

`source: edstem thread #100`

**Question:** A student couldn't locate the examples of HCD data gathering methods in the playbook that were mentioned during the Milestone 3 assessment lecture.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Mark initially suggested the relevant sections were likely Concept Testing (pages 65–68) and Usability Testing (pages 76–78) in the playbook, noting the PDF may have extraneous "Old Slides" junk at the end that can be safely ignored. He also pointed to examples discussed during HCD lectures from both terms. He subsequently shared a response from Carolyn identifying the following specific tools and how each can be used for HCD data gathering:

- **Stakeholder network** (slide 30) — validate key stakeholders with them directly; check for any missing ones.
- **Persona** (slide 33) and **empathy map** (slide 34) — communicate stakeholder motivations and needs to see if they resonate; clarify context, timing, and value of your proposition.
- **Journey map** (slide 35) — show stakeholders the current-experience map to check accuracy and capture corrective feedback on-the-spot.
- **Blueprint** (slide 37) — confirm understanding of all stakeholder interactions, including front-stage and backstage elements.
- **Affinity mapping** (slides 41–44) — capture quotes throughout the project and identify emerging themes.

Carolyn's broader note: most tools and methods in the DRP Playbook can be used creatively to gather feedback from relevant people.
