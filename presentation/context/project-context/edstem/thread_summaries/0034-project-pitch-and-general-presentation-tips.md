# #34 — Project Pitch and General Presentation Tips

`source: edstem thread #34`

**Question:** This is a staff announcement providing guidelines and general tips for the Friday project pitch sessions (Milestone 1).

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Guidelines and tips for the pitch recording and presentation:

- The pitch must be exactly **3 minutes** (strict limit), include a **slideshow or animations**, and have a **voice-over** that is not artificially sped up.
- During the live pitch sessions, team members should briefly **introduce themselves** (name and group only, no project details) before playing the recording; this introduction does **not** count toward the 3-minute limit.
- Slides: avoid overcrowding with text; check text-background contrast in presentation mode and have someone else judge readability.
- Presentation: practise beforehand in the delivery format; don't hold scripts or phones; don't memorise a single fixed script — rehearse multiple alternatives instead; don't rely on a teammate to advance slides (it rarely works, and you can edit it out).
- HCD: don't refer to personas explicitly as "personas" — introduce them naturally (e.g. "Meet Bob. Bob is a builder…").
