# #81 — Implementation

`source: edstem thread #81`

**Question:** Does the final digital implementation need to fully cover all features shown in the mock-ups, or is it acceptable to have mock-ups that are more advanced than the coded touchpoint?

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** It is totally fine — and expected — for mock-ups/prototypes to be significantly more advanced than the actual digital touchpoint at this stage. The Walking Skeleton assessment criteria are written with this in mind and do not require full implementation of everything in the mock-ups. Next week groups are expected to bring their digital touchpoint closer to the mock-ups; at that point it may become faster to iterate directly on the digital touchpoint for the core interaction, though returning to paper prototyping for entirely new interactions is still recommended to avoid writing unnecessary code.
