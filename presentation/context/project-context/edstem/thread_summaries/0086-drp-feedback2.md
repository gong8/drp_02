# #86 — DRP Feedback^2

`source: edstem thread #86`

**Question:** Students raised concerns about the DRP Marking Tool feedback: (1) red/yellow boxes mixing assessment failures with advisory notes feels misleading; (2) some feedback is too vague to be actionable without knowing why current work is deficient; (3) the "keep doing" section was left blank for at least one group.

**Answer (Jamie Willis):** The red/yellow signage is intentional — the points do not necessarily mean marks were deducted; the focus is not supposed to be on that. The intent is to ensure groups keep important things in mind through to the final presentation, since past cohorts have forgotten earlier learnings by the end. Happy to discuss further on Tuesday.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Three responses across the sub-threads:
- On the mixed advisory/failure boxes: would like to discuss in more detail the following week (not available during Tuesday Clinic hours); has follow-up questions.
- On vague feedback: asked students to bring specific examples to Clinic Hours for further clarification; acknowledged the concern and invited dialogue.
- On the blank "keep doing" section: tutors are recommended to write something in all three categories where possible, but only when the content is actionable — they avoid writing filler that isn't useful.
