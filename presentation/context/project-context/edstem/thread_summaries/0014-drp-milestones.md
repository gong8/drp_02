# #14 — DRP Milestones

`source: edstem thread #14`

**Question:** A student asked whether DRP milestones are simply submission deadlines, or whether they involve in-person meetings (like code reviews) where students present their work.

**Answer (Jamie Willis):** The milestones are not code reviews (the process matters, not the code itself), but they do involve weekly meetings with assigned tutors where students present that week's progress. Meeting times are set, but groups will not know their slot until the week of the second milestone (for milestones 2–4).

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Groups will have a small amount of choice over when their weekly meeting takes place, though not as much flexibility as with PintOS or WACC. He advises keeping those days entirely clear of external commitments.
