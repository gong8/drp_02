# #83 — Explicit prototype walktrough required?

`source: edstem thread #83`

**Question:** A student asked whether teams are required to walk tutors through their prototype live during the Friday Milestone 2 review, or whether documentation of the prototype and feedback/iterations is sufficient — noting their paper prototypes had become messy since testing.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Tutors do want to see how mock-ups and prototypes were used during user interviews, but the preferred way to demonstrate this is via a video recording of the actual interviews rather than a live walkthrough during the meeting. Live walkthroughs are still permitted but are less authentic than seeing the prototype used with real target users, and may also be a slower way of presenting the work and process.
