# #28 — Lab Zones Map

`source: edstem thread #28`

**Question:** This is a staff announcement addressing how students should communicate their physical location when requesting support during Clinic Hours.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** When asking for help during Clinic Hours, students should provide their location using the Lab Zones Map. The zone names draw from Solar System, Star Wars, and Norse Mythology themes: Solar System names correspond to the main labs, Norse realm names to the back-labs, and anything else to more unusual locations. Students must include both their zone name and their desk/machine number in their Teams query, since the zone system alone is not precise enough to locate someone.
