# #5 — Introduction to Human-Centred Design Workshop

`source: edstem thread #5`

**Question:** This is a staff announcement about an upcoming Human-Centred Design (HCD) Workshop for COMP50010 students.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** A Human-Centred Design Workshop will be held on Friday 20th March from 11:00 in HXLY 308 (with HXLY 311 available as breakout space). Key points:
- Attendance is expected.
- Preparation material, slides, and design template documents will be uploaded to the [COMP50010 Materials page on Scientia](https://scientia.doc.ic.ac.uk/2526/modules/50010/materials) by mid-week before the workshop; hard copies will be provided on the day.
- The materials will not make sense in isolation — attending the workshop is strongly encouraged to get full value.
- The workshop serves as a meaningful dry-run of the DRP project in one day; past groups who engaged thoughtfully tended to have a much stronger start to the Summer term project.
