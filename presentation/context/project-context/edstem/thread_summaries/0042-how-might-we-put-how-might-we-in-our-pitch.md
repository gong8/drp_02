# #42 — how might we put 'how might we' in our pitch?

`source: edstem thread #42`

**Question:** A student asked whether the "How Might We" (Opportunity Statement) needed to be included in the Elevator Pitch presentation, or whether submitting it in the milestone assessment schedule was sufficient.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The Opportunity Statement must be included in the Elevator Pitch presentation — suggested as the final slide. It should be displayed long enough for the audience to read it (not just briefly flashed), and a good way to ensure this is to have it read aloud during the recording. Additionally, content uploaded to the milestone assessment schedule will not be assessed; that submission is only to give staff a general idea of what the team is working on.
