# #57 — Lecture recordings from this morning

`source: edstem thread #57`

**Question:** A student asked why the lecture recordings from that morning were not visible on Panopto, even though they had been able to watch the first two hours live.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The recordings needed a few minutes of manual processing to trim idle chit-chat (e.g. while getting a guest connected to Zoom) before being published. He also noted surprise that the lectures had been live-streamed, as he had not been informed of this. The recordings were made available later that day, with the extraneous material before and after each session trimmed out.
