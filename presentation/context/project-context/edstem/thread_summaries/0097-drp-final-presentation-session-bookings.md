# #97 — DRP Final Presentation Session Bookings

`source: edstem thread #97`

**Question:** This is a staff announcement about how and when groups should book a slot for the DRP Final Presentation, and what information needs to be filled in on the booking form.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Groups must book a presentation slot within their allocated Assessment Stream using the DRP Final Presentations Tab of the Assessment Schedules spreadsheet. Key details:

- The spreadsheet was opened for student editing at 13:00 on Wednesday 3rd June.
- All groups must select a slot by **7pm on Friday 12th June**; after that, unbooked groups will be assigned a slot.
- You must pick a slot within your Tutor Team's allocated Assessment Stream — no exceptions. Invalid slots are highlighted red.
- When booking, remove your group from the "Unallocated" list at the top of the page.
- The **Project Title** (the final name of your digital touchpoint) and a **Project Pitch Leaflet** (inserted as an image in the spreadsheet cell) must be added by the booking deadline. The leaflet is not directly assessed here but can also be submitted as part of the DRP Project Documentation due Friday 19th June.
- There are 20 slots per stream for 18–19 groups, so checking the timetable before it opens is advised to secure a preferred slot.
- The final version of the leaflet is not due until 19:00 on Friday 19th June (submitted to Scientia); what goes on the spreadsheet is only a draft for potential Q&A feedback. The booking sheet will be locked after 12th June to allow staff to resolve errors.
