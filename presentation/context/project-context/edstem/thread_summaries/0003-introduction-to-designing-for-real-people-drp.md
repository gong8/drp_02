`source: edstem thread #3`

**Question:** A student asked whether the DRP introductory session and the following DRP/HCD lectures would be recorded and available on Panopto.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The DRP Introduction and both Introduction to HCD lectures will be recorded as usual, though in-person attendance is strongly encouraged as the sessions are highly interactive. The HCD Workshop will not be recorded as it is a hands-on/practical session.
