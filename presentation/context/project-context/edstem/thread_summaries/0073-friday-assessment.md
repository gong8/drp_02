# #73 — Friday Assessment

`source: edstem thread #73`

**Question:** A student asks whether the Friday assessment requires a formal presentation of findings, or whether it is more informal (e.g. a conversation about what has been done).

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The session is conversational in structure but students should come with prepared content (slides are a reasonable way to organise this). Two options are available: present prepared content for 5–10 minutes first, or ask staff to guide the session through the criteria. Either way, the first 1–2 minutes must be used to give a quick reminder of the problem context (ideally through the lens of a persona) and the (likely revised) opportunity statement ("how might we…"). See also thread #63 for additional advice.
