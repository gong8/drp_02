# #85 — DRP Milestone 2 Feedback

`source: edstem thread #85`

**Question:** Staff announcement addressing the release of Milestone 2 feedback and offering guidance on how groups should present their work in future milestone review sessions.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Milestone 2 feedback could not be uploaded to Scientia and was instead emailed as a PDF attachment. Individual milestone marks will not be released until after the project ends and moderation is complete. DRP Clinic Hours (Monday and Tuesday the following week) are available for further clarification. The tutor team noted strong cohort engagement with agile and human-centred design processes. Key tips for future milestone review sessions:

- **(0) Be polite** to each other, staff, and end users; do not get defensive about critical feedback.
- **(1) Project context (1-2 min):** Open each session with a concise problem statement, especially after any pivot, so tutors can follow your work.
- **(2) Show your week's work (5-10 min):** Be visual ("Show, Don't Tell"); include both positive and critical stakeholder feedback; authentic evidence is most convincing.
- **(3) Q&A / tutor feedback (at least 5-10 min):** Tutors may interject during the presentation to give timely feedback.
- **Suggested structure:** Planning (user stories, not features) → User Interaction (how feedback was gathered) → Feedback Impact (show evolution of mock-ups/app). A useful format is: [old mock-up] → user feedback quote/photo/video → [new mock-up].
- **Preparation tips:** Record or take notes in stakeholder interviews; avoid biased/leading questions; use recorded footage sparingly (max 10-20 seconds in a 20-minute session).
