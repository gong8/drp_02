# #63 — DRP Milestone {2,3,4} Assessment Format/Advice

`source: edstem thread #63`

**Question:** What is the format and content expected for DRP Milestone 2, 3, and 4 review sessions, and what specifically should students prepare for each milestone's assessment criteria?

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The milestone review sessions are 20 minutes, held on-site in HXLY 221, 225, or 311, with two tutors present. The rough structure is:

- ~1 min (unassessed) reminder of project theme: setting, personas, problem, opportunity statement.
- Up to 15 mins (strictly timed; aim for ~10 mins) covering the 5 assessment criteria for the week — ~2 mins per criterion. Show work and evidence (producibles, video snippets, etc.).
- ~10 mins discussion of general project progress, tips, and next steps.
- ~5 mins for tutors alone to agree on marks and type up feedback.

**Milestone 2 criteria:**
- **Mock-ups:** Show latest mock-ups; assessed on level of interaction for core functionality.
- **User interaction with mock-ups:** Show (ideally via video) users exploring core functionality and being probed on their experience.
- **Feedback and impact:** Show iteration — early mock-up, user feedback, revised mock-up. Aim for 2–3 iterations; prefer deep refinement of one interaction over shallow refinement of many.
- **CI/CD pipeline:** Show the .yaml (or equivalent); expect a live push of a small FE update through the pipeline to update the deployed touchpoint in real-time.
- **Deployed digital touchpoint:** Demo a minimal but end-to-end complete, context-relevant interaction (not a landing page, login, or "hello world"). End-to-end means FE and BE are connected — an action on the FE is sent to/saved by the BE and visualised in the FE again.

**Milestone 3 criteria:**
- **Plan for the week:** Overall theme, affected user journeys, specific user stories — not just a feature list.
- **Deployed touchpoint:** Demo a few interactions; should be more evolved than last week (beyond walking skeleton, approaching MVP); design must be meaningful and context-relevant; prefer focused, usable core interactions over many unfocused features.
- **User interaction:** Show users exploring the digital touchpoint; must include use of specific HCD data-gathering techniques (see HCD Lectures and DRP Playbook).
- **Feedback and impact:** Show iterative development — early version, feedback, changes. Cherry-pick the most impactful changes; prefer depth over breadth.
- **Thin-Slicing:** Similar to the above two criteria; focus on technical details of how BE/DB changes supported FE changes across the week.

**Milestone 4 criteria (same as M3 except the last two):**
- **Plan, deployed touchpoint, and feedback/impact:** Same expectations as Milestone 3.
- **Impact asset:** Show the impact asset and explain its context; focus on direct impact on users and indirect impact on wider community/lives; frame it around the idealised future version of the project (realistic, not grandiose).
- **Objective metric:** Present one quantitative, objective metric tracked during the week; show how it varied (e.g. via a graph) relative to changes in the touchpoint; metrics should augment, not replace, user testing.
