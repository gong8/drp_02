# #106 — Week 4 presentation and final presentation

`source: edstem thread #106`

**Question:** A student asks how the Week 4 milestone presentation differs from the final presentation the following week.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The two sessions are quite different:
- The Week 4 session is a milestone review (format detailed in thread #63 and its follow-ups): limited to 10–15 minutes of presentation, more discursive in nature, and assessed on process rather than outcomes.
- The final presentation the following week is a 20-minute session where groups present the final state of their project for the full 20 minutes without interruption. Further details were posted in thread #110.
