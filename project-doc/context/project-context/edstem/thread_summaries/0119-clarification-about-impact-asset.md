# #119 — Clarification about impact asset

`source: edstem thread #119`

**Question:** A student asked for clarification on what "Positive wider impact" means for Milestone 4 — specifically whether it includes wider stakeholder and societal groups beyond the project's direct target users.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The "positive wider impact" refers to the *indirect* impacts and benefits of your work, not just the direct impact on your target users. It should consider your project's indirect stakeholder groups.
