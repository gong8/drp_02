# #21 — New DRP Week 1 Playbook

`source: edstem thread #21`

**Question:** The announcement addresses the release of a new Week 1-focused version of the DRP Playbook and the conditions under which tutor support will be available during Clinic Hours.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** A new Week 1 version of the DRP Playbook has been released (flagged as a follow-up to thread #9). Students are strongly encouraged to use it to guide their project work in Week 1. Tutor support during Clinic Hours will not be provided to groups until they have prepared at least a draft research plan — coming without one is compared to asking for help with no context provided. Feedback on the document is welcome. (A file-extension bug that prevented the PDF from opening was reported by students and fixed by Mark shortly after.)
