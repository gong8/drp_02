# #133 — DRP Milestone 4 Feedback

`source: edstem thread #133`

**Question:** Staff announcement informing students that Milestone 4 feedback has been released and outlining what to do in preparation for final presentations.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Milestone 4 feedback has been uploaded to Scientia via the DRP Marking Wiki feedback return process. Tutors noted a significant improvement in work quality across the board. Students have the weekend and Monday to polish their projects before final presentations. Students should upload their project pitch leaflet or impact asset to the [Final Presentation Demonstration Schedule](https://imperialcollege.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docteaching/pages/152415816/DRP+-+Final+Presentation+Demonstration+Schedule) as soon as possible.

**Answer (Jamie Willis):** A student reported that Milestone 4 feedback showed "You will be assigned to a group soon" rather than actual feedback. The issue was caused by an EdTech change that blocked group formation on Scientia when there is no submission, which in turn prevented group creation; only group leaders could initially see the feedback. Jamie flagged this to Ivan Procaccini to unblock group-accept via the API. The issue was fully resolved within the hour — all students can now see their feedback.
