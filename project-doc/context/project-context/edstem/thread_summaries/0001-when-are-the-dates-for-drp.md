# #1 — When are the dates for DRP?

`source: edstem thread #1`

**Question:** A student asked when the DRP dates were, noting nothing was visible on Scientia yet, and guessed the project might run 25th May – 19th June.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The project runs across two terms:

- **Introductory lectures** (Human Centred Design) take place in week 11 of the Spring term (visible on CELCAT; minor schedule changes possible before term starts).
- **Main project period** is Summer term weeks 4–8: 18th May – 19th June. The full milestone assessment schedule will be uploaded to Scientia before the Spring term begins.
- **Milestone review sessions** (in-person progress meetings, mandatory attendance) fall on the first four Fridays: 22nd May, 29th May, 5th June, and 12th June.
- **DRP Final Presentations** are on Tuesday 16th and Wednesday 17th June. All group members must attend their own group's chosen session. Attendance of other sessions throughout the week is optional but encouraged; all presentations will be recorded via Panopto and shared to the class over summer.
- **After presentations:** Formal teaching and assessment ends by 17th June, but on the afternoon of Friday 19th June there are optional (non-mandatory) in-person sessions: a "Preparing for Year 3" talk and Q&A (will be recorded), a Year 2 Townhall feedback meeting, and an End-of-Year Social with free food and soft drinks.
