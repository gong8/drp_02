# #136 — Two personas with similar journeys

`source: edstem thread #136`

**Question:** A student asks whether it is messy or confusing to introduce two personas from the same stakeholder group at the start of a presentation, given that their journeys and needs are very similar.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** Introducing both personas early in the presentation is the better approach — it avoids a character appearing "out of nowhere" later in the story. If their initial journeys are very similar, you can present both personas and then note the shared journey, showing just one as the example rather than walking through each separately.
