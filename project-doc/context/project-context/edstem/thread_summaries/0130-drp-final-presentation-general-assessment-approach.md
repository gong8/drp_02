# #130 — DRP Final Presentation - General Assessment Approach

`source: edstem thread #130`

**Question:** This staff announcement addresses how marks are awarded across all assessment criteria in the DRP Final Presentation, in anticipation of student questions before the dedicated Q&A session on Monday.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** All assessment criteria use a 0–3 scale:
- 0 — missing (even after the Q&A session)
- 1 — poor / incomplete discussion
- 2 — good / expected discussion
- 3 — excellent / perfect discussion

Most groups giving a solid presentation will receive mostly 2s, with 3s and 1s reserved for notably strong or weak elements. No criteria are tick-box marked — all are judgements of *how well* something was done. For example, the timing mark is not solely about finishing within the 18–21 minute window, but also about avoiding rushing or padding, both of which the assessment panel will notice.
