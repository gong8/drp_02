# #9 — DRP Playbook

`source: edstem thread #9`

**Question:** This is a staff announcement about a resource provided to support the DRP module — specifically the availability and purpose of the DRP Playbook.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The RCA prepared a "DRP Playbook" to guide students through their research and design journey. It is available on the [Scientia materials page for COMP50010](https://scientia.doc.ic.ac.uk/2526/modules/50010/materials) under Module Support Material. The Playbook is divided into four stages covering the full process from starting research through to delivering the final project solution; its templates and tools are suggestions, and students are encouraged to adapt them to their project's needs. It is a living document and feedback is welcome at any time. A follow-up comment (posted 2026-05-15) noted that a refined full version would be uploaded to Scientia soon, with a week-1-focused version available immediately for use the following week.
