# #27 — DRP Clinic Hours Protocol

`source: edstem thread #27`

**Question:** This is a staff announcement describing the protocol for DRP Clinic Hours sessions held on Huxley Level 2.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** All four main Huxley Level-2 lab rooms (219, 202, 206, 210) are booked for DRP Clinic Hours; students are encouraged to attend on-site. Support is managed via the COMP50010 MSTeams "DRP Clinic Hours Queue" channel for the day. Key points:

- Attend in person on Huxley Level 2 and be logged into Teams to request support.
- Post a support request with your location and a brief issue summary into the queue channel; do not add follow-up replies as this moves your post to the bottom.
- Do not post more than 5 minutes before the session starts — early posts will be ignored until the end of the queue is reached.
- Tutors work top-to-bottom through the queue; they use a heart reaction (❤️) to signal a request is being handled and a thumbs-up (👍) to signal it is resolved.
- No queue-jumping by physically approaching tutors — all requests must go through Teams.
