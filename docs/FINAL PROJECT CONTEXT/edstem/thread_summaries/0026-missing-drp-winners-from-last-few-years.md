# #26 — Missing DRP winners from last few years

`source: edstem thread #26`

**Question:** A student asked why the Best Second Year Group Projects page on the Imperial website has not been updated since 2023, and suggested it would be useful to see more recent winners given the growing relevance of AI.

**Answer (Mark Wheelhouse):** The page is outdated due to lack of time and difficulties with the Imperial CMS. Initial intention was to upload content from the last couple of years within the week, but it was subsequently discovered that the carousel structure used for the page had been deprecated by the Imperial CMS, requiring a complete rebuild — which is not feasible in the near term. In the meantime, project recordings from 2025 have been uploaded to Panopto. A proper fix is hoped for over the Summer.
