The Reconnection Issue
Speculative 2028 cover
No organiser
Nobody had to be the bossy one.
Every plan is sent anonymously, so it reads as the group's idea, not one person's ask. "It's more of a consensus than one person giving a proposal."
Insight 4 - validated by Tom, interview
No public maybe
We killed the word "maybe".
One early hedge used to cascade and sink the whole plan. Now it is a blind Yes or "Can't make it" - no public hedging, no contagious doubt.
Insight 2 - Luke, interview
Shared, not dumped
The chasing stopped falling on one friend.
Most groups leaned on one specific friend to push every plan. Spread anonymously, the labour of coordinating no longer lands on the same person every time.
Insight 3 - survey, 43 responses
The honest bit
One thing we are still wrestling with.
Naming a friend in "I'll go if [people]" felt awkward even when private. We are testing ranking importance instead of naming names - a tension we are designing around, not hiding.
Insight 9 - Felicity, iteration interview
