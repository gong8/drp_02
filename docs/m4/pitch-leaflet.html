Imperial × Royal College of Art · Team DRP_02 · 2026

Turn "we should hang out" into a firm plan.

BeThere is a group meetup app that removes the two things that quietly kill plans: the organiser nobody wants to be, and the public "maybe" everyone hides behind. One anonymous meetup, the group votes, the top pick wins, then everyone commits in a blind, timed RSVP. It comes together, or it disappears with no trace.

Who it's for Friend groups of about four to six - the most common group size in our survey - who coordinate in a group chat and routinely watch plans fizzle. Larger groups are harder, not easier, to pin down (survey).

The Problem

Group plans die in the gap between "we should meet up" and an actual time and place. Nobody wants to be the organiser (the effort is asymmetric), and a public "maybe" lets everyone hedge. One early hesitation makes the whole group's certainty collapse, so the plan quietly fizzles in a silent chat.

How might we Help a group of friends turn a loose intention to meet into a committed plan, without anyone being the organiser, and without the social pressure of a public "maybe"?

How it works
1

Send

One meetup to the group, sent anonymously. No organiser.

2

Vote

Members +1 times and activities. Counts show, names never.

3

Lock

Top time and activity win when voting closes.

4

Commit

Blind, timed RSVP: Yes / Can't make it / "I'll go if". It clears or fizzles.
